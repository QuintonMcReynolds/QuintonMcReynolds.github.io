Privacy Policy – Cuetip's Hideout

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Cuetip's Hideout (the "Game"), developed by Quinton McReynolds ("we", "us"), handles information. The Game is designed for a general audience that includes children under 13, and we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, photos, or contacts. The Game does not require an account and has no chat or social features.

Game progress and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. All ad requests are tagged as child-directed, so AdMob serves only non-personalized, family-appropriate ads and does not use the information below for interest-based advertising or remarketing. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address (used for general location only), device type, operating system version, and ad interaction data. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How information is used

Any technical information processed by our ad provider is used only to show ads, measure their performance, and protect against fraud. We do not sell information and do not share it with anyone else.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to store. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally saved game data from your device.

Security

Data handled by our ad provider is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
